Silvia Bombardini is a writer and occasional film curator and a PhD candidate in visual sociology at Goldsmiths, University of London. 
She is Associate Lecturer in Contextual and Theoretical Studies at the London College of Communication, and Associate Lecturer in Fashion and Design Products at the Royal College of Art. 
Her PhD explores shoplifting as a feminist practice in the early 1900s, by way of the inventive clothes shoplifters wore. 
Her PhD project is part of the Politics of Patents: Re-imagining Citizenship via Clothing Inventions 1820-2020 research project which has received funding from the European Research Council (ERC) under the European Union's Horizon 2020 research and innovation programme (Grant Agreement No. 819458). 
She has written for international magazines including Modern Weekly China. Instagram @silviabombardini.
